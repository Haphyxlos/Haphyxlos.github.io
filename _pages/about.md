I am **Haphyxlos**. My research focuses on the **interpretability and mechanistic analysis of large language models** — understanding how knowledge, reasoning abilities, and computation are structured inside transformer models, and how such structure can be safely edited or transferred.

Topics I care about include: sparse and low-rank structure in model weights, cross-task transfer of internal circuits, behavioral analysis of RL-finetuned models, and evaluation methodology for interpretability claims.
